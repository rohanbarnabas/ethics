# The CFA Level 2 Ethics Map

One page, all of ethics. Companion to the Ethics videos on Rohan's Mind Mapped.

## The idea

Ethics is a promise (honest service at an honest cost), delivered to the client and the employer, between the law and the Code, with transparency, all resting on trust in the system and the person. Every standard hangs off that. Learn the map and each detail has a place.

## Use it

- **Click a node** to open its card, with a line back to the node. Close it with the button on the card's top strip. On a phone it opens as a sheet.
- **Drag** to move around. **Pinch or Ctrl + scroll** to zoom. Drag a card by its top strip to move it.
- **Fit** resets the view. **Open all** opens every card (it becomes **Close all**). **Hide standards** clears the numbered standards (e.g. III(C)) so only the big picture shows.
- **Own notes** on each card: your own words, your own example. Saved in this browser only.

## Each card has

| Section | Use it for |
|---|---|
| The principle | The idea in one line |
| Examples | What a violation looks like in practice |
| Learned in isolation | The tempting misreading, where it leads, and the right reading |

## How to study with it

1. **Before questions:** open each branch once, in the order of the video.
2. **When you get a question wrong:** find its node. Read "What it looks like". Is that the misreading you made? If yes, that is your mistake, so write it in Own notes.
3. **Later:** close everything and redraw the map from a blank page. Open the cards and check what you missed.

Principles are paraphrased, not quoted from the CFA curriculum. Always check against the official text.
